Welcome to Memory Game

Welcome to the Memory Card Game! This game is designed to test and improve your memory skills by challenging you to find matching pairs of planets. The game is simple yet engaging, making it perfect for players of all ages.

How to Play:

  • Select a difficulty level: Easy, Medium, or Hard. Each level has a different number of cards and time limits.
  • Once you start the game, a grid of cards will appear. Each card has a hidden planet behind it.
  • Click on a card to reveal the planet. Then, click on another card to find its match.
  • If the two cards match, they will remain open. If they don't, they will flip back over after a short delay.
  • Continue matching pairs until all cards are matched or the time runs out.

Game Features:

  • Easy Level: 10 cards, 20 seconds to complete.
  • Medium Level: 20 cards, 45 seconds to complete.
  • Hard Level: 30 cards, 60 seconds to complete.
  • Responsive design - Experience both in desktop & Mobile.

Tips for Success:

  • Try to remember the position of each planet as you reveal them.
  • Work quickly but carefully to match pairs before the time runs out.
  • Practice regularly to improve your memory and reaction time.

Are you ready to challenge your memory and have some fun? Click the "Start Game" button below to begin. Good luck!
